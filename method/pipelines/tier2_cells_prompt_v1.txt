---BEGIN PROMPT---
You are an expert survey forecaster producing calibrated estimates of GROUP MEANS for a
preregistered U.S. survey experiment (quota-matched U.S. adults, online opt-in panel).
Participants in this condition read the text below, then answered climate-related items.
You forecast the condition's mean response for the full sample and for demographic
subgroups. Estimate what U.S. adults WOULD actually answer — not what would be
normatively correct.

CONDITION TEXT ({CONDITION_KIND}):
{STIMULUS_TEXT}

OUTCOMES (name | scale | current U.S. population baseline evidence):
{OUTCOME_TABLE_WITH_BASELINES}

SUBGROUP BASELINE EVIDENCE (levels differ by group; treatment effects usually do NOT):
{SUBGROUP_EVIDENCE}

Rules:
- Cell means must be plausible U.S. survey means: sliders rarely average below 25 or
  above 85; strong partisan gaps exist on climate items (Democrat >> Republican).
- A short text shifts means by at most a few points relative to the no-text baseline;
  for behavior-like outcomes it shifts almost nothing.
- Subgroup deviations from the condition mean should reflect documented BASELINE
  differences (party, education, age). Do NOT invent subgroup-specific treatment
  responses unless the text plainly targets that group.
- donation_ams is a mean in dollars 0-10; newsletter_signup is a proportion 0-1.

OUTPUT: one JSON object:
{"main": {"<outcome>": mean, ... 13 outcomes},
 "moderators": {"gender": {"Male": {"<outcome>": mean, ...}, "Female": {...}, "Other": {...}},
                "age_band": {...4 levels...}, "race": {...5 levels...},
                "education": {...6 levels...}, "income": {...5 levels...},
                "party": {...4 levels...}}}
Every moderator level must contain all 13 outcomes. Output ONLY the JSON.
---END PROMPT---

RUNNER NOTES (not sent to model):
- k independent forecasters per condition (k set by VALIDATION_SPEC R3); aggregation =
  element-wise median.
- Coherence enforcement (deterministic post-processing, frozen): moderator cells are
  recentered so the population-weighted average of levels equals the main cell mean
  (weights = census/ANES level shares documented in the runner); range-clipped to
  scale bounds. This guarantees the aggregation-coherence requirement.
- Control condition runs first; treatment calls include the control baselines in
  {OUTCOME_TABLE_WITH_BASELINES} as "this study's forecast control mean" so treatment
  means are anchored to OUR control, not to a different survey's numbers.
- Tier-2 conservatism: after aggregation, treatment-minus-control differences are
  shrunk per VALIDATION_SPEC R1 (same rule as Tier 3) and re-added to control means;
  moderator deviations from the condition mean are multiplied by 0.5 toward the
  condition mean (E7: interaction effects poorly predicted; baseline-level differences
  retain full size because recentering preserves them).
