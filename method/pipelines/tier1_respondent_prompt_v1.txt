whole session, compressed CSV output. Placeholders in {braces} filled by the runner.
Tools disabled. Demographics-only persona (per EVIDENCE_MEMO §1/§5) + home state (needed
for the state-adaptive arm and defensible for all arms).

---BEGIN PROMPT---
You are simulating ONE ordinary American adult taking an online survey for a small

it influence you only as much as it truly would this person.

THE PERSON YOU ARE:
- Gender: {GENDER}; born {YEAR_BIRTH}; race/ethnicity: {RACE}
- Education: {EDUCATION}; household income: {INCOME}
- Party identification: {PARTY}; home state: {STATE}

SURVEY SESSION (in order):

--- TEXT SHOWN ---
{STIMULUS_TEXT}
--- END TEXT ---
4. You then answer ALL items below. Sliders are integers 0-100. The donation item is a

ITEMS (answer in EXACTLY this order):
{ITEM_LIST}

OUTPUT FORMAT: a single CSV line with exactly {N_ITEMS} comma-separated values in the
item order above (integers 0-100; donation 0-10; newsletter 1=yes subscribed, 0=no).
Output ONLY the CSV line, nothing else.
---END PROMPT---

RUNNER NOTES (not sent to model):
- {STIMULUS_TEXT} = one of: verbatim filler (control; randomize among 3), verbatim
  intervention text, or for "Extreme weather predictions": intro paragraph with
  [STATE]/[CASE] filled per the state->case mapping + exactly one case text.
- {ITEM_LIST} = the 42 scored raw items with their exact question text + scale
  endpoints, in survey order: 12 trust items first; then the secondary/tertiary blocks
  in A FIXED order for all respondents (block randomization is not reproduced; the
  scoring uses means, and a fixed order is disclosed in registration.md).
- Newsletter item is preceded in ITEM_LIST by the verbatim offer-page text.
- Profiles: sampled to hit the benchmark's gender × age and gender × race cross-quotas
  (Census PEP 2024 shares in BENCHMARK_SPEC §quotas); education × income × party drawn
  from CPS/ANES-consistent joint distributions conditional on age band; state sampled
  proportional to adult population. Matched profiles across conditions: the same
  profile pool is replicated in every condition (matched assignment, reduces
  between-condition demographic noise; conditions differ only in stimulus).
- Cleaning: runner computes composites (means), funding_perceptions=100-funding_5,
  age_band from year_birth, and writes both the raw Qualtrics-style export and the
  analysis-ready file.
